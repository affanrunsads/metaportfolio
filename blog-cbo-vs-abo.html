CBO vs ABO Strategies for E-Commerce

One of the most common questions I get from mentees and clients is: "Should I use CBO or ABO?" The answer isn't a simple yes or no—it depends entirely on what phase of the campaign lifecycle you are in.

When to Use ABO (Ad Set Budget Optimization)

ABO gives you strict control over how much money is spent on specific audiences or creatives. This makes ABO the ultimate tool for Testing.

When you are testing 5 different creatives against a broad audience, using a CBO will often result in the algorithm dumping 90% of the budget into the one creative that gets early clicks, starving the others. With ABO, you can force Facebook to spend a set amount (e.g., $20/day) on each creative, ensuring you get statistically significant data to crown a true winner.

When to Use CBO (Campaign Budget Optimization)

Once you have found your winning audiences and winning creatives using ABO, it's time to graduate them to a CBO campaign. CBO is the ultimate tool for Scaling.

By placing your proven winners into a CBO, you are giving Meta's machine learning algorithm the freedom to distribute your budget in real-time to the ad set that is performing the best on any given day. If Ad Set A is having a slow day, Meta will automatically funnel the budget to Ad Set B, stabilizing your overall account CPA.

The Hybrid Approach

In my agency, we run a hybrid system. We have a perpetual ABO testing campaign running at a low budget. Every week, the top-performing ads are duplicated into our high-budget scaling CBO campaign. This ensures our CBO is constantly being fed fresh, proven winners, preventing ad fatigue.
