Tackling the High Return Rate of Cash on Delivery (COD) in Pakistan

Cash on Delivery (COD) is the lifeblood of e-commerce in Pakistan, accounting for the vast majority of all online transactions. However, it brings a massive headache for business owners: incredibly high return rates and fake orders.

The Hidden Costs of COD Returns

When a package is returned (RTS - Return to Shipper), you lose the delivery fee you paid to the courier, your inventory is tied up for weeks, the packaging is often damaged, and worst of all, you paid Meta for that acquisition. A return rate over 20% can wipe out your net margins completely.

Strategies to Minimize Returns

1. Mandatory Order Verification: Never dispatch an order on autopilot. Implement an automated WhatsApp API or SMS verification system. If they don't confirm the order, it doesn't ship. For high-value items, a quick verification phone call works wonders to filter out non-serious buyers.

2. Fast Dispatch and Transparency: Buyer's remorse sets in quickly with impulsive social media purchases. If you take 3 days to dispatch, the customer will likely refuse the parcel when it arrives on day 7. Dispatch within 24 hours, and send a tracking link via WhatsApp immediately.

3. Analyze and Blacklist: Keep a database of phone numbers and addresses with a history of returns or fake orders. Shopify apps and local courier portals often provide risk analysis. Furthermore, analyze return data to identify specific cities or rural areas with consistently high return rates and exclude those zip codes from your Meta Ad targeting.
