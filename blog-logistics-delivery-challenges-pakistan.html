Navigating Logistics and Last-Mile Delivery Challenges in Pakistan

You can have the best product and the most profitable Meta Ads campaigns, but if the courier fails to deliver the package to the customer's doorstep, you lose money. Logistics in Pakistan, particularly last-mile delivery, remains one of the largest bottlenecks for e-commerce growth.

The Reality of Last-Mile Delivery

Issues range from riders marking parcels as "Customer Refused" without ever making a phone call, to extensive delays in rural and tier-2 cities. These inefficiencies directly feed into high Return to Shipper (RTS) rates, destroying profitability.

Tactics to Take Control of Your Logistics

1. Diversify Your Courier Partners: Never rely on a single logistics provider. Different couriers have different strengths. Courier A might be excellent in Karachi and Lahore, while Courier B has better penetration in rural Sindh or KPK. Use a logistics aggregator or maintain active accounts with multiple companies to route parcels based on their geographic strengths.

2. Proactive Tracking and Customer Communication: Don't leave the customer in the dark. Integrate automated WhatsApp tracking updates. Send a message when the order is dispatched, and crucially, send an alert on the day of delivery telling the customer to keep their phone accessible. This reduces missed deliveries drastically.

3. The "Fake Attempt" Escalation Protocol: If a customer complains that they were available but the tracking shows "Consignee Not Available," escalate immediately. Build a relationship with your courier account manager and establish a protocol to re-attempt delivery the next day, bypassing the standard return process.
